# MixMaster — privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

MixMaster works out floor-coating mixes, jobs and stock for a flooring company. It is published
by **[publisher: ConWiC Oy, or the person publishing it]**. This policy covers the MixMaster app
on Google Play.

## The short version

MixMaster has no account with us, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we run no server.
What you put in it stays on your phone, unless your company shares it: then it goes to **your
company's own server**, which your company sets up and controls, and to nobody else.

## What stays on your phone

Products and recipes, jobs with their floors, rooms and coats, tasks, notes, photos, blueprints,
recorded mixes, stock and orders, and your settings. Photos are made smaller when they are added
(2048 pixels on the long side).

## When your company shares its data

A company can keep one set of data on every phone in it. Its data then goes, over an encrypted
connection (https), to the company's own server: its website, or its own Google account. That is:

- the names the owner gives each person, and what each may change
- products, recipes, jobs (including the client's name and the site address the company types
  in), floors, rooms, coats, tasks, notes, recorded mixes, stock, deliveries and orders
- photos and blueprints added to jobs, and the name of whoever wrote a note or marked an order

The company decides what goes in and who sees it, and is responsible for that data. We do not
receive it and cannot see it.

## Leaving, and deleting

- **A worker** leaves under Settings → Company → **Leave company**: the company's data is deleted
  from the phone.
- **The owner** can remove anyone under People. The next time that phone connects, MixMaster on
  it is emptied, and their entry is deleted from the company's server.
- **Settings → Empty the app** deletes everything MixMaster keeps on the phone. Uninstalling does
  the same.
- Data on the company's server is deleted by the company. To have it deleted, ask the company.

## Permissions

| Permission | What for |
| --- | --- |
| Internet, network state | The company's server, when the company shares its data |
| Notifications, alarms, full-screen alerts, vibration, start at boot | The mixing timer ringing when a batch is done, with the phone locked; the stock count reminder |
| Fingerprint / face | The app lock, if you turn it on. The phone checks it; MixMaster never sees it |
| Uninstall | Offered only on a phone its company has taken off |
| Nearby devices, and location on Android 12 and older | Pour Day, the break-time game, playing together with phones close by. MixMaster does not read or keep your location |

## Other things it uses

- **Backups** you export are saved where you choose, and go nowhere else.
- **The guide's voice** is your phone's own text-to-speech, reading the guide's own captions.
- **If MixMaster crashes**, the details are shown on the phone. They go only where you send them.
- **Pour Day, the game inside,** collects nothing. Playing together, phones close by exchange the
  day being played and the name each player types, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, through Google Play
  services ([Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).

## Children

MixMaster is a work tool, not made for children.
