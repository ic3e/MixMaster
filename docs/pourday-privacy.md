# Pour Day — privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Pour Day is a game about a day on a concrete site, published by **[publisher: ConWiC Oy, or the
person publishing it]**. This policy covers the Pour Day app on Google Play.

## What Pour Day collects

Nothing. Pour Day has no account, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server. It does
not ask for the internet. Nothing about you or your phone is sent to us or to anyone else.

What the game keeps, it keeps on your phone: your settings (sound, voices, subtitles, controls)
and the name you type for playing together. Uninstalling Pour Day removes them.

## Playing together

"Play together" connects phones that are close by, directly, over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, using
Google's Nearby Connections (part of Google Play services). No internet is used. What passes
between the phones is the day being played, and the name each player typed (16 letters at most,
"Worker" if left empty), which the other players see.

To find phones nearby, Android asks for the **Nearby devices** permission, and on Android 12 and
older for **location**, because older Android versions only allow Bluetooth and Wi-Fi scanning
with it. Pour Day does not read or keep your location. It asks only when you host or join a day,
and you can say no and still play alone.

Google Play services, which makes the connection, is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Voices

The lines are read out by your phone's own text-to-speech. Only the game's own lines are passed
to it.

## Children

Pour Day is not made for children under 13: it has swearing, and mentions alcohol and smoking.
