Left thumb walks. Right thumb looks. Hold the big button to work on whatever glows orange, or on the slab with the tool in your hands. Open the van: the tools wait on its ramp. Look at one and press Pick up; the same button puts it down. Wait lets the concrete do its thing. You can't go home before it's 95% hard. Slide your thumb on the big button to look round while you work — that is how you steer the trowel. II or the back gesture pauses.
